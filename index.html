Information

About Us

Hidden Kusina is a professional Filipino catering business based in Avondale, Auckland. We are a hidden gem: a small kitchen cooking big, authentic flavours for homes, workplaces and celebrations across the city.

Why “Hidden Kusina”?

In Tagalog, kusina means kitchen. Ours is tucked away in Avondale, which is where the name comes from. We are the kind of place people discover by word of mouth and keep coming back to.

What we do

We cook traditional Filipino dishes the way they are made at home, from slow-simmered classics and crowd-pleasing snacks to desserts and refreshing drinks. We cater for small gatherings, family fiestas, corporate events and everything in between.

Certified and registered

Hidden Kusina is a registered food business operating under a verified food safety programme in line with the Food Act 2014. Our kitchen is registered and our processes are certified, regularly verified and held to the standards required in New Zealand. Our registration and certification details are available on request.

Our promise

  • Fresh ingredients, prepared with care and cooked to order.
  • Safe food handling, clear allergen information and hygienic preparation.
  • Honest pricing and reliable, professional service.
